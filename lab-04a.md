# Tacos al Pastor

A classic Mexican recipe with marinated pork and pineapple.

## Ingredients

- 500g of pork meat (shoulder or loin)
- 2 guajillo chiles
- 1 fresh pineapple
- Corn tortillas
- Cilantro and white onion (for topping)
- Lime wedges
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Cooking Steps

1. Marinate the pork with the guajillo chiles, achiote paste, and spices for at least 4 hours
2. Cook the meat in a hot skillet or on a vertical spit until charred on the edges
3. Warm the corn tortillas on a comal or dry pan
4. Slice the meat thinly and serve on the warm tortillas
5. Top with diced pineapple, cilantro, white onion, and a squeeze of lime

**Important Cooking Tip:** *Marinate the pork for a minimum of 4 hours — overnight is even better for maximum flavor.*

**Important Cooking Tip:** Always serve the tacos immediately while the tortillas are still warm so they stay soft.

# Academic Integrity Policy

## Purpose

To maintain a high standard of academic integrity across the Seneca community and provide the foundation for research, teaching, learning and working practices.

## Scope

This policy applies to current students and employees.

## Key Definitions

### Academic Integrity

> Within an academic environment, "a commitment, even in the face of adversity, to six fundamental values: honesty, trust, fairness, respect, responsibility and courage." From these values flow principles of behaviour that enable academic communities to translate ideals to action to ensure the credentials granted to students accurately reflect their demonstrated knowledge and abilities.

### Cheating

**Cheating** is obtaining or attempting to get, or aiding another person to obtain, credit for work or improvement in performance evaluation by dishonest or deceptive means. Examples include self-plagiarism, impersonation, falsification, taking individual credit for group work, unauthorized collaborations and aids, and **undeclared AI use**.

### Contract Cheating

**Contract cheating** is when a student submits academic work created by a third party as if it were their own for academic credit.

### Plagiarism

**Plagiarism** is using another individual's, group's or organization's work and presenting it as one's own, without properly citing the source.

### Unauthorized Aid

**Unauthorized aid** is using non-approved supports to complete coursework or assessments, including peer collaborations, artificial intelligence technology, the internet, smartphones, etc.

## Policy

### 1. General

- Seneca upholds a learning community that values academic integrity, honesty, fairness, trust, respect, responsibility and courage.
- Seneca is committed to delivering high-quality education and teaching excellence while supporting a positive, equitable and inclusive learning environment.

### 2. Academic Integrity Program

- Academic integrity is fundamental to ensure the academic credentials granted by Seneca accurately demonstrate the knowledge and learning of students.
- The integrated nature of the program requires that policy and procedure, development and training for faculty, along with training sessions and student life programming, be aligned with institutional philosophies.

### 3. Responsibilities

- Seneca is responsible for providing information and resources to students about academic integrity.
- Students are responsible for informing faculty of any circumstance affecting their academic performance.
- If a student witnesses or suspects a violation, they can report it following the procedures in Appendix A.

### 4. Sanctions

- Academic integrity sanctions will be applied according to the severity of the violation committed.
- Before final decisions are made, the student's record should be consulted for aggravating and/or mitigating factors.
- Should a violation result from or combine with a violation of Seneca's Student Code of Conduct, the matter will be investigated through the Student Code of Conduct processes.

## Appendix A: Academic Integrity Procedure

| Action | Duration | Responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Report witnessing or suspicion of a violation to the designate in your school/program | As soon as possible | Faculty, invigilator, student, support staff |
| Inform the student of the suspected offence and discuss informally | As soon as possible | Faculty, invigilator, support staff |
| Collect and retain the assessment and related evidence | Within 5 days of reported violation | Faculty, invigilator, support staff |
| If no offence occurred, inform the student — no further action | Within 2 business days | Faculty |
| If an offence occurred, inform the student and commence formal process | Within 5 business days | Faculty |
